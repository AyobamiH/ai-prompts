# Channel Adaptation Guide

## Shared source record

Every channel asset must point to the same underlying record:

```yaml
story_id: stable-id
verified_claims: []
prohibited_claims: []
evidence_links: []
asset_ids: []
audience: named-audience
desired_outcome: named-outcome
```

If a channel version needs a claim absent from this record, update and reverify the source record before drafting.

## Threads

Useful shapes:

- one sharp observation plus a genuine question;
- compact incident with believed state and discovered state;
- opinion that invites informed disagreement;
- short lesson followed by useful replies.

Avoid mini white papers, stacked CTAs, identical LinkedIn copy, and automatic product mentions in replies.

## Instagram

Use visual narrative:

1. painful moment or category statement;
2. what appeared true;
3. separate states or checks;
4. evidence result;
5. consequence;
6. question or next step.

For reels, show the outcome first, then the check. For carousels, keep one idea per frame and validate text legibility at phone size.

## Reddit

Start from the post author's exact problem. Give the useful answer before mentioning a product. Read current subreddit rules. Disclose affiliation in plain language when the product is relevant. Avoid serial link drops, manufactured questions, copied campaign language, and unsupported claims of independent community adoption.

## LinkedIn

Translate the incident into delivery, engineering leadership, governance, cost, risk, or customer impact. Preserve technical evidence, but explain why it changes a decision. Avoid generic AI futurism and feature inventories.

## Facebook

Match the group context. A developer group can receive technical evidence. A founder or local-business group needs customer and operational outcomes. Do not post the same promotional asset into unrelated groups.

## GitHub

GitHub is proof rather than just reach. Keep examples reproducible, releases current, installation honest, issues responsive, and documentation consistent with runtime. Link to exact commits, runs, or releases when a social claim depends on them.

## Google Business Profile

Use one local customer problem, a purpose-built image, concise outcome language, an appropriate CTA, and a validated tracked destination. Use `google-business-profile-post` for staging and publication.

## New-account opening sequence

For an empty parent-brand account:

1. complete identity, bio, link, category, logo, and banner;
2. publish the category introduction;
3. publish a visual model of how the products or workflow relate;
4. publish a real incident;
5. ask a discussion question that helps refine audience language.

Do not create separate product accounts merely to repeat these posts.
