# Brand Profile Operations

Make the profile complete, credible, visually coherent, and attached to the correct brand without inventing business facts.

## Before editing

1. Confirm the authenticated account, page owner, existing audience, brand, and target market.
2. Apply `product-brand-architecture` when deciding whether to create a new company page, product page, or social identity.
3. Inventory every current field and asset. Preserve live information that is correct and useful.
4. Verify factual fields such as founding year, address, phone, website, industry, availability, and legal name before saving them.

## Complete the profile

Prepare a frozen profile specification containing:

- display name and handle;
- one-sentence positioning;
- full about text;
- category or industry;
- website and action link;
- location or service area;
- specialties or services;
- messaging and contact policy;
- logo and banner source assets;
- fields deliberately left blank with reasons.

Save in bounded sections when a large form hides validation failures. Re-read each saved section before moving on.

## Asset handling

Derive current dimensions and limits from the provider. Inspect actual file metadata before upload. If a pre-cropped banner fails repeatedly, retry with the full-resolution artwork and the provider's crop tool. Do not delete a working live asset until a replacement is known to save.

## Completion proof

Verify the public profile, not only the editor. Report fields and assets that are live, failed, pending review, or intentionally unchanged. Never claim a logo or banner was replaced from a successful upload alone.
