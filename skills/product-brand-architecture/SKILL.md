# Product Brand Architecture

Create an architecture that makes product relationships legible without collapsing trust boundaries or fragmenting an early audience.

## Decision order

1. Define each product's actual job, user, maturity, trust role, and commercial relationship.
2. Decide whether a neutral parent is needed. Prefer a parent when sibling products have complementary roles or one must remain independent from the other's claims.
3. Separate company name, category language, product names, and campaign taglines. One phrase should not carry all four jobs by default.
4. Research live name, domain, company, product, and trademark collisions. Domain availability is time-sensitive and checkout is the final authority.
5. Allocate domains by role: parent home, primary product homes, category education, and only justified defensive redirects.
6. Apply the account proliferation gate before creating social or company pages.
7. Produce a staged migration and redirect plan. Preserve existing audiences and backlinks where they still fit the brand.

## Trust-boundary rule

Do not place an independent verifier under the execution product it evaluates unless the architecture explicitly preserves independence. Do not write copy implying automatic certification between sibling products without operational evidence.

## Account proliferation gate

Create a separate page or social account only when most of these are true:

- distinct audience and buying intent;
- distinct recurring content supply;
- stable product availability and onboarding;
- enough operator capacity to maintain it;
- clear discovery value that outweighs audience fragmentation.

Otherwise use the parent account and product pages, highlights, or site sections.

## Output

Return an architecture map, naming rationale, domain roles, purchase priority, redirect plan, account topology, staged separation gates, collision risks, and facts that still require legal or registrar confirmation.
